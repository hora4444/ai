Chapter 1 General

 

Article 1. Definition 

Terms used in these conditions of carriage shall be defined as follows; 



1. “JEJUAIR” means Jeju air, Incorporated. 



2. “Passenger” means any person, except members of the crew, carried or to be carried in an aircraft with the consent of Carrier.



3. “Carriage” which is equivalent to transportation, means carriage of passenger and/or baggage by air, gratuitously or for reward. 



4. “Carrier” means an air carrier that also includes the carrier that can issues a ticket and is obliged to carry passengers or their baggage under this ticket or to perform related other services for air carrier.



5. “Flight tickets” are passenger and baggage claim or electronic tickets (e-tickets) issued by the carrier and include all terms and conditions, coupons that are part of the ticket. 

A. Composition of passenger ticket

1) Flight Coupon: The coupon is a part of a flight ticket that specifies a specific section to be transported by the passenger, and is exchanged for a boarding pass when you check-in.

2) Passenger coupon / passenger receipt: This is a part of flight ticket which can prove the contract of carriage with the passenger.



6. “Conjunction Ticket” means two or more tickets concurrently issued to a passenger and which constitute a single contract of carriage.



7. "E-Ticket” means an electronic ticket confirmation (e-Ticket confirmation) and an electronic coupon issued by an airline or other airline electronic ticket issued on behalf of an airline. 



8. " Electronic ticket confirmation (E-ticket confirmation, e-Ticket itinerary / Receipt)” is a document that identifies passengers' names, flight and notices for passengers who will use e-ticket (e-ticket) to take a plane.



9. "An electronic coupon” is a coupon which identify a specific segment to be transported by the passenger, or related information stored in the airline's database.



10. "All freight charges and Fee” means the air fare, fuel surcharge, airport usage fee, and all charges set by JEJUAIR for air transportation.



11. " A fuel surcharge” is a charge to save the airline's additional costs for fuel price increases.



12.“Airport Service Charge” means service fees charged by the government authorities or the airport authorities and collected by the Carrier.



13. "The published fare is a discounted fare set by JEJUAIR for air transportation.



14. "Baggage” means the passenger's goods, supplies and other personal items which is prepared from the passenger for comfort or convenience, and includes all checked and carry-on baggage unless otherwise specified.



15.“Checked Baggage” means a piece of baggage, surrendered by a passenger with a valid ticket, that the Carrier has taken custody of and has issued a baggage check tag for.



16.“Unchecked Baggage” means baggage carried and taken by a passenger and kept in cabin.



17.“Excess Baggage” means baggage in excess of the free baggage allowance permitted by the Carrier.



18.“Baggage Tag” means the portion of the ticket issued by the Carrier to confirm the carriage of a passenger’s checked baggage. It consists of a baggage identification tag attached to the checked baggage and a baggage claim tag handed over to the passenger.



19.“Excess Baggage Receipt” means a receipt issued by JEJUAIR as documentary evidence that the relevant excessive baggage charge has been paid.



20.“Adult” means a passenger who is 12 years of age or over. 



21.“Child” means a passenger who is 2 years of age or over but under 12 years of age.



22. “Unaccompanied minor” means a passenger over the age of 5 years who is not accompanied together with their guardian or an adult passenger when they take a plane.



23.“Infant” means a passenger who is under 2 years of age.



24. "Cancellation penalty” is a charge imposed under the refund policy when the ticket is refunded.



25.“Check-in deadline” means the time defined by JEJUAIR for a passenger to complete Check-in and receipt of Boarding Pass. 



26. “Tariff” means the fare, rates and charges applicable to the carriage of passengers and baggage by JEJUAIR and also includes the rules and procedures related to the carriage, which form part of carriage clause.



27. "Force majeure” refers to cases where it is inevitable due to special and unpredictable circumstances (such as weather conditions, airport and etc.) that JEJUAIR cannot control.



28.“Destination” means the ultimate stopping place according to the contract of carriage.



29.“Stop-over”, which is a synonym of the stop of travel, means that a passenger stops the trip intentionally in the point between starting point and destination under Carrier’s prior consent.



30.“United stated of America or The united states or The USA” means, unless otherwise specified, the area comprising the 51 contiguous federated states, the Federal District of Columbia, Alaska, Hawaii, Guam, Puerto Rico, St. Croix and St. Thomas of the Virgin Islands, American Samoa, The Canal Zone, Canton, Midway and Wake islands.



31.“Round trip”, which is equivalent to a return journey, means travel from one point to another and return by the same air route used outbound whether or not the fares outbound and inbound be the same, or travel from one point to another and return by an air route different from that used outbound, for which the same normal through, one-way fare is established. 



32. “Damage” includes death, injury, delay, loss or other damage of whatsoever nature arising out of or in connection with carriage or other services performed by carrier incidental thereto.



33.“Miscellaneous charges order” means a document issued by a carrier or its agent, requesting issue of an appropriate Passenger Ticket and Baggage Check or provision of services to the person named in such document, and in case of electronic environment, it means “Electronic miscellaneous document” (hereinafter, “EMD”).



34.“Convention” means the Convention for the Unification of Certain Rules Relating to International Carriage by Air, signed at Warsaw, October 12, 1929, (hereinafter called “the Warsaw Convention”) or that Convention as amended at the Hague, September 28, 1955 (hereinafter called “the Warsaw Convention as amended at the Hague, 1955”), or “the Convention for the Unification of Certain Rules Relating to International Carriage by Air” amended at Montreal May 28, 1999, (hereinafter called “the Montreal Convention”), whichever may be applicable.



35.“International carriage other than that defined by the convention” means any carriage which is not international carriage as defined by the Warsaw Convention or the Warsaw Convention as amended at The Hague, 1955, or the Montreal Convention as amended at Montreal, 1999, but in which, according to the agreement made by the parties, the place of departure and the place of landing are situated in more than one country. As used therein, the term “country” which is equivalent to “state” includes all territory subject to its sovereignty, suzerainty, mandate, trusteeship or authority. 



36.“SDR (Special Drawing Right)” means the Special Drawing Rights defined by the International Monetary Fund.(hereinafter called “IMF”).



37.“French gold francs” means French francs consisting of 65 1/2 milligrams of gold at the standard of fineness of nine hundred thousandths.



38.“Days” means calendar days, including Sundays and legal holidays; provided that for the purpose of notification, the day upon which the notice is dispatched shall not be counted; and that for purpose of determining duration of validity, the balance the day upon which the ticket is issued, or flight commenced shall not be counted.
